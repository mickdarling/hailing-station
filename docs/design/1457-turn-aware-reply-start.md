# Let the owner finish before starting a spoken reply

## Delivery plan (2026-11-27)

Phase 1 of #1457 is a phone-local MVP. When sequence zero at the FIFO head is ready to play, the terminal holds it while its own pre-mask capture tap detects sustained public near-end speech. Start with 40 ms of speech to open the hold and 120 ms of measured quiet to release it. Both values need hardware validation. Show “Waiting for you to finish” during the hold. Cap the added wait at 5 s from the first otherwise-playable instant. The hold keeps every admitted reply in FIFO order without dropping, cancelling or resending it. Privacy, Stop and Talk always take precedence, including at the ceiling.

Phase 1 adds no wire contract, child event or telemetry. The RightyO `turn_activity` event and host relay (stages 1–5 in section 6) follow only if replays or owner use show the local veto is insufficient, for example with AirPods or a far-field Mac capture. Implementation waits for the owner's answers to the questions in section 7.

## 1. Decision and evidence

**Proposed decision.** Keep receiving the answer. Let the terminal that will play it wait at the first playable audio frame if the owner's turn is open or likely to continue. Start once the turn closes, with a **five-second ceiling** on this additional wait. While waiting, keep the microphone available for the next fragment. After playback starts, retain the existing interruption behavior. This works even when an agent has already answered fragment three.

**Reported on hardware.** One note became five turns in about 30 seconds. The answer to turn three played over turn four. On the iPhone speaker with voice-processing I/O (VPIO), overlapping speech mostly arrived in one- or two-word pieces. AirPods or waiting avoided the symptom in the owner's trials. The division of losses between the digital mask and VPIO attenuation is **(inferred)**. The report does not establish which Smart Turn configuration was active. Issue #1457 · `docs/rightyo-swimlanes.md:119–133` (draft).

**Two different acceptance measures.** Count unintended playback starts even if transcription still produces five turns. Separately measure whether better endpointing reduces fragments and dispatches. #1440 reports short, often unlabelled first-word fragments and a possible word cut at a boundary. A delay at the speaker cannot restore samples already lost. #107's roughly 300 ms added-latency target concerns end-of-turn recognition, whereas five seconds here is an exceptional playback ceiling. Hailing Station #1440 · RightyO #107 · `docs/audio-decision-map.md:66–70`.

**Proposed wire contract for the conditional follow-up.** One optional stdout JSONL event, `turn_activity`, with two states, `open` and `ended`. In addition to the normal session, sequence, version and emission-time envelope, carry only the state and `observed_through_ms`, the end position of the public audio supporting it. A fixed v1 lease of **1,500 ms** makes a TTL field unnecessary. `open` means “defer automatic speech here.” It covers speaking and likely continuation without claiming who spoke. No text, PCM, tokens, probabilities, speaker labels, names, embeddings, request IDs or persistent identity. Keep `p` and silence diagnostics local. Existing stdout envelope and event publication are in `docs/tool-api.md:20–37` and `src/rightyo/tool_events.py:916–935,1109–1150`.

**Proposed emission rules.**

- Open from fresh public voice in `LiveProcessor._frame`, upstream of transcription and dispatch. Prefer current, non-overlapping owner evidence when available. During a public exchange, provisionally protect an unlabelled onset such as #1440's first fragment. Known other speakers and playback/echo alone cannot renew an owner continuation. The absence of a label grants no owner, privacy-control, dispatch or interruption authority. The existing owner lookup is in `src/rightyo/prototype.py:1221–1246`.
- Carry the same open state across a Smart Turn `wait`, plausible merge gap, or forced split with continuing voice. Refresh at most once per second, only on newly processed public evidence. End on a confirmed finish with no newer onset or pending continuation, or on an observed quiet/merge deadline when the model is unavailable. Do not wait for agent dispatch. Fence late ASR/merger callbacks by local window generation so an old final cannot end a newer turn.
- Wire a typed callback through `prototype.py` into the ordered `SpeechEvents` publisher. Enable it only for a negotiated live Ambient child. For example, haild sets `RIGHTYO_TURN_ACTIVITY=1` for that spawn, and a supporting child advertises top-level `turn_activity.version=1` on `session.started`. Without the host opt-in, emit neither. Old children can ignore the new environment option. Precedents are `src/rightyo/tool.py:54–72,366–391` and HS `RightyoChildProcess.swift:22–35`. The current HS event allowlist has no turn-activity event. `RightyoInputEvent.swift:283–298`.
- Fail closed on disclosure. Check the **whole supporting span** and the current privacy generation both when producing and when draining an advisory. Suppress on private or unknown privacy, exceptions, non-live provenance in production, stale binding, or uncertain playback overlap. Purge pending hints on privacy entry without emitting a speech-derived closing edge. Clear state on shutdown. Coalesce unsent refreshes before assigning sequence, reserve capacity for substantive events, and skip the advisory under pressure. The existing publisher has queue limits and the host currently caps remembered event fingerprints at 4,096. New heartbeats must not exhaust either budget. `src/rightyo/live_audio.py:2064–2079` · `src/rightyo/tool_events.py:926–935` · HS `RightyoInputConsumer.swift:678–712`.

## 3. From haild delivery to the first sample

**Current path.** haild validates the reply, finds the unique request-owning peer, applies its final publication and privacy checks and enqueues the frames. Requestless delivery has separate last-input, pinning and recipient rules. The receiving socket passes final text and audio to the connection store. Station connects that store to `ReplyPlaybackController.ingest`. The controller stores sequences, presents the reply, and drains the contiguous FIFO head. On sequence zero, `beginPlaybackIfNeeded` claims the output. The guarded player can raise masking before scheduling, and the PCM player schedules buffers and calls `node.play()`. **The last clean place to wait is before claiming the head or scheduling its first frame.** HS `HostReplyDelivery.swift:1320–1342,1603–1635,1654–1715` · `HostConnection+Protocol.swift:44–69` · `HostConnectionStore.swift:393–405` · `Apps/Hail-iOS/RootView.swift:103–112` · `ReplyPlaybackController.swift:127–156,178–224` · `ReplyPlaybackController+Proactive.swift:39–49` · `AmbientReplyEchoGuard.swift:524` · `PCM16AudioPlayer.swift:60–81`.

**Proposed host and terminal responsibilities.**

1. **haild validates and relays.** Admit only a newly accepted event from the negotiated live child, bound to the current run, stream and owning connection, while public. Use a dedicated admitted-advisory result. The existing `consume` Boolean also returns false for valid non-request events and duplicates. Strictly validate raw keys, version, enum, monotone sequence and stream position. Reject malformed data without forwarding any part. Recheck run, owning connection and privacy under the final send fence. Serialize notices with that peer's audio. On replacement, advance the stream revision even if the socket survives. Do not block a reply publisher, acknowledge a request twice or send a notice to the target adapter. HS `RightyoInputConsumer.swift:625–637,678–712` · `RightyoAmbientPipeline.swift:300–334` · `WebSocketListener.swift:1703–1714`.
2. **Keep age and scope across the socket.** Negotiate a host-to-terminal `turn_activity_v1` control through hello. Relay state with the active stream ID, a monotone revision for that stream and a recent capture-segment sequence corresponding to the child's observation. The app can then expire it against **its own capture time**, even if the socket stalls. Track only a short in-memory mapping from actual public child-input offsets to admitted source segments, and from those segments to local capture times. Unknown mappings, withheld audio, inserted silence, restarts and stale generations are ineligible. Subtract age already spent in transit. Neither replaying a packet nor relabelling the same observation with a new revision extends the lease. An older end cannot clear a newer local onset. A lost end expires naturally. Without a negotiated signal, the terminal uses existing wire delivery, its local start decision and all existing protections. Main already has sequence-aware input and distinguishes actual writes from queued/withheld bytes. HS `AmbientAudioStreamer.swift:117,268–289` · `RightyoAmbientPipeline.swift:394–434` · `RightyoStdinWriter.swift:27–42` · `HostConnection+Protocol.swift:17–41,87–92`.
3. **Station makes one start decision.** Admit and display text and buffer audio normally. When the first contiguous frame would otherwise start, check the current device mic. If the follow-up relay is enabled, a fresh host lease can also veto the start. A short, sustained public onset just before scheduling can veto start while the host notice is in flight. Use the capture tap before masking, only on live, eligible near-end input while output is quiet. For replay, use 40 ms of sustained speech-like energy to open and 120 ms of measured quiet to release the local veto, with the same 1,500 ms stale-sample limit. Validate those starting values on hardware. A separate reader of the pre-mask tap preserves the existing proactive-update quiet rule. Missing buffers cannot prove quiet or renew a veto. Sound without attribution can cause a brief false hold **(inferred)**. Use the ordinary FIFO stream and show “Waiting for you to finish”. While held, leave sequence zero unconsumed, `activeKey` unset for this reply, and its playback-start, reply mask and segment-yield window unraised. The existing tap and measured-quiet clock are in HS `AmbientReplyEchoGuard.swift:669–675` and `ProactiveAudioActivity.swift:59–95`.
4. **Release exactly once.** The first otherwise-playable instant sets an immutable monotonic deadline, **T + 5 s**. Measured quiet or expiry of stale local evidence wakes the normal drain. With the relay enabled, a turn end or lease expiry also wakes it. Recheck all applicable vetoes on each wake. At the deadline ignore *only* the turn veto for that reply, even if fresh speech continues. Recheck privacy, authority, Stop, Talk, user controls, route readiness and FIFO ownership. Renewals, repeated drains, duplicates, a second fragment or another reply cannot move this deadline. A generation-checked timer and a recheck on wake/foreground handle idle or suspended apps. Explicit Stop or privacy revocation wins a tie. The ceiling bounds delay added by this feature, independently of a user pause or OS suspension.
5. **Retain the answer.** Use the existing stream, ID, sequence order, final marker and replay bookkeeping. The hold itself has no drop, cancel, text-only or resend outcome. Keep first-audio *arrival* timers meaningful and suspend the head's missing-final watchdog only during this intentional wait. Preserve existing deduplication. Avoid `isPaused` for this purpose because that path can discard entire queued prefixes under its retention budget. Size retention for the largest admitted burst during five seconds. If more data can arrive, retain the unsent suffix at the publisher against its existing reply ID and pace audio independently of privacy/Stop controls. Require a multi-reply pressure replay before enabling. HS `ReplyPlaybackController.swift:128–153,161–175,202–215` · `ReplyPlaybackController+Idle.swift:78–101,115–133` · `ReplyAudioProblem.swift:122–144`.

Keep publication and actual playout distinct. A waiting reply must not arm the child's playback-overlap clock, an “asked” window or a device playback-start report. Existing echo matching observes delivered frames and estimates playout, while optional legacy child feedback follows device diagnostics and may be dropped. Include the extra delay when testing echo matching at the eventual start/end and ensure that the owner's fourth fragment is not treated as self-echo merely because answer three was published. HS `HostReplyDelivery.swift:1731–1743` · `RightyoAmbientPipeline.swift:694–716` · `WebSocketListener.swift:817–831,1053–1086` · `docs/audio-decision-map.md:92–95`.

## 7. Questions for the owner

1. Is five seconds the right point to let **one queued answer** start during sustained speech? A shorter ceiling reduces waiting. A longer ceiling protects more of a dictated note.
2. For the first word, should a fresh unlabelled onset receive the proposed provisional protection even if a nearby person can occasionally delay an answer? Waiting for verified ownership leaves the start of some thoughts uncovered.
3. Should AirPods use the same timing as the speaker? The proposal keeps both at five seconds and preserves the selected output.
4. Should ordinary acknowledgements wait along with the answer, and should a deliberate Replay count as an explicit request to hear it immediately? The proposed defaults are yes to both, subject to privacy and Talk.
5. If several fragments each earned an answer, is playing each admitted answer in FIFO order acceptable? This design retains them all. Combining or withdrawing superseded answers would be a separate decision about requests.
