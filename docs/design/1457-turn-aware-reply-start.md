# Let the owner finish before starting a spoken reply

## Delivery plan (2026-11-27)

Phase 1 of #1457 is a phone-local MVP. Only an agent answer that the phone can bind to a request from existing reply fields is eligible. When its sequence zero would otherwise play, the terminal holds the answer while its own pre-mask capture tap detects sustained public near-end speech. Phase 1 never holds requestless audio. Acknowledgements, privacy confirmations, status clips, notices and requestless agent answers all keep baseline timing. Start with 40 ms of speech to open the hold and 120 ms of measured quiet to release it. Both values need hardware validation. Show “Waiting for you to finish” during the hold. Cap the added wait at 5 s from the first otherwise-playable instant. For eligible answers that remain authorized, the local pressure replay must demonstrate complete FIFO playback without drop, cancellation or resend. Privacy, Stop and Talk always take precedence, including at the ceiling. Privacy entry invalidates every held agent reply. Covering requestless Ambient answers would require a trusted host-side marker and falls outside phase 1.

Phase 1 adds no wire contract, child event or telemetry. The RightyO `turn_activity` event and host relay are follow-up work only if replays or owner use show that local capture misses speech, for example with AirPods or a far-field microphone. Implementation waits for the owner's answers to the questions in section 7.

## 1. Decision and evidence

**Proposed decision.** Keep receiving the answer. For a proven request-owned answer, let the terminal wait at the first playable audio frame if the owner's turn is open or likely to continue. Start once the turn closes, with a **five-second ceiling** on this additional wait. While waiting, keep the microphone available for the next fragment. After playback starts, retain the existing interruption behavior. Whether this could protect turn four in the reported case depends on the answer's request binding.

**Public report.** One note became five turns in about 30 seconds. The answer to turn three played over turn four. [Issue #1457](https://github.com/mickdarling/hailing-station/issues/1457)

**Separate, unverified owner observations.** The owner reports that overlapping speech mostly arrived in one- or two-word pieces during iPhone speaker trials with voice-processing I/O (VPIO). In the owner's AirPods and wait-until-finished trials, the symptom went away. The division of losses between the digital mask and VPIO attenuation is **(inferred)**. The report does not establish which Smart Turn configuration was active.

**Two different acceptance measures.** Count unintended playback starts even if transcription still produces five turns. Separately measure whether better endpointing reduces fragments and dispatches. #1440 reports short, often unlabelled first-word fragments and a possible word cut at a boundary. A delay at the speaker cannot restore samples already lost. #107's roughly 300 ms added-latency target concerns end-of-turn recognition, whereas five seconds here is an exceptional playback ceiling. Hailing Station #1440 · RightyO #107 · `docs/audio-decision-map.md:66–70`.

**Proposed wire contract for the conditional follow-up.** One optional stdout JSONL event, `turn_activity`, with two states, `open` and `ended`. In addition to the normal session, sequence, version and emission-time envelope, carry only the state and `observed_through_ms`, the end position of the public audio supporting it. A fixed v1 lease of **1,500 ms** makes a TTL field unnecessary. `open` means “defer automatic speech here.” It covers speaking and likely continuation without claiming who spoke. No text, PCM, tokens, probabilities, speaker labels, names, embeddings, request IDs or persistent identity. Keep `p` and silence diagnostics local. Existing stdout envelope and event publication are in `docs/tool-api.md:20–37` and `src/rightyo/tool_events.py:916–935,1109–1150`.

**Follow-up emission rules.**

- Open from fresh public voice in `LiveProcessor._frame`, upstream of transcription and dispatch. Prefer current, non-overlapping owner evidence when available. During a public exchange, provisionally protect an unlabelled onset such as #1440's first fragment. Known other speakers and playback/echo alone cannot renew an owner continuation. The absence of a label grants no owner, privacy-control, dispatch or interruption authority. The existing owner lookup is in `src/rightyo/prototype.py:1221–1246`.
- Carry the same open state across a Smart Turn `wait`, plausible merge gap, or forced split with continuing voice. Refresh at most once per second, only on newly processed public evidence. End on a confirmed finish with no newer onset or pending continuation, or on an observed quiet/merge deadline when the model is unavailable. Do not wait for agent dispatch. Fence late ASR/merger callbacks by local window generation so an old final cannot end a newer turn.
- Wire a typed callback through `prototype.py` into the ordered `SpeechEvents` publisher. Enable it only for a negotiated live Ambient child. For example, haild sets `RIGHTYO_TURN_ACTIVITY=1` for that spawn, and a supporting child advertises top-level `turn_activity.version=1` on `session.started`. Without the host opt-in, emit neither. Old children can ignore the new environment option. Precedents are `src/rightyo/tool.py:54–72,366–391` and HS `RightyoChildProcess.swift:22–35`. The current HS event allowlist has no turn-activity event. `RightyoInputEvent.swift:283–298`.

Suppress on private or unknown privacy, exceptions, non-live provenance in production, stale binding, or uncertain playback overlap. Purge pending hints on privacy entry without emitting a speech-derived closing edge. Clear state on shutdown. ## 3. From haild delivery to the first sample

**Current path.** haild validates the reply, finds the unique request-owning peer, applies its final publication and privacy checks and enqueues the frames. Requestless delivery has separate last-input, pinning and recipient rules. The receiving socket passes final text and audio to the connection store. Station connects that store to `ReplyPlaybackController.ingest`. The controller stores sequences, presents the reply, and drains the contiguous FIFO head. On sequence zero, `beginPlaybackIfNeeded` claims the output. The guarded player can raise masking before scheduling, and the PCM player schedules buffers and calls `node.play()`. **The last clean place to wait is before claiming the head or scheduling its first frame.** HS `HostReplyDelivery.swift:1320–1342,1603–1635,1654–1715` · `HostConnection+Protocol.swift:44–69` · `HostConnectionStore.swift:393–405` · `Apps/Hail-iOS/RootView.swift:103–112` · `ReplyPlaybackController.swift:127–156,178–224` · `ReplyPlaybackController+Proactive.swift:39–49` · `AmbientReplyEchoGuard.swift:524` · `PCM16AudioPlayer.swift:60–81`.

**Ambient request binding.** The host can put a request reference in an Ambient prompt. If the publisher uses it, the host checks request ownership and the phone receives the reference in the reply descriptor. If the publisher omits it, haild can route the answer to the last input device or another eligible Ambient recipient with `requestID == nil`. Routing to the correct phone supplies no request binding. The listener test exercises both forms on the streaming device. #1457 gives no descriptor for turn three. On the requestless path, phase 1 leaves that answer at baseline and cannot prevent the reported overlap. Covering it requires a host-side marker identifying agent answers, outside phase 1. HS `WebSocketListener.swift:548–562` · `ReplyCommand.swift:219–228` · `HostReplyDelivery.swift:1603–1635,1654–1715` · `AmbientWiringReplyReferenceTests.swift:55–69`.

**Phase 1 on the phone**

1. **Establish request ownership before considering a hold.** For example, on the current connection the phone receives the host-admitted final answer text with a non-nil `ReplyDescriptor.requestID`. 2. **Check immediately before start.** Admit text and buffer audio normally. Observe live public near-end PCM at the pre-mask tap while output is quiet. Check that evidence immediately before the first contiguous frame would otherwise start. For replay, use 40 ms of sustained speech-like energy to open the hold, 120 ms of measured quiet to release it and a 1,500 ms stale-sample limit. Validate those starting values on hardware. A separate reader of the pre-mask tap preserves the existing proactive-update quiet rule. Missing buffers cannot prove quiet or renew a hold. Sound without attribution can cause a brief false hold **(inferred)**. Show “Waiting for you to finish”. Park only the eligible answer before it claims the player. 3. **Release exactly once.** The first otherwise-playable instant sets an immutable monotonic deadline, **T + 5 s**. Measured quiet, stale evidence or the deadline can request a drain. The timer callback only requests release. Before handing a privacy or Stop notice to the store, the connection marks its receive callback as pending. A release waits for pending controls to finish, including when the timer runs first. At the deadline the `@MainActor` drain may ignore only this answer's turn hold. Immediately before scheduling, reread the store's `duckMode` and the held generation on that actor. Recheck authority, Stop, Talk, user controls, route readiness and output ownership. Keep the check, output claim and first schedule in one actor turn without suspension. A notice first received after a sample was scheduled cannot retract that sample. Fresh speech, duplicates, another reply and repeated drains cannot move the deadline. Check the timer's generation on every wake, including foreground. The ceiling bounds the wait added by the hold, independently of user pause or OS suspension.
4. **Retain authorized audio on the phone.** Keep the existing reply and stream IDs, sequences, final marker, deduplication and replay bookkeeping. Keep first-audio *arrival* timers running and suspend the head's missing-final watchdog only during the intentional wait. The existing `isPaused` path can discard queued prefixes under pressure, so give the hold its own state. The local multi-reply pressure replay in section 5 decides whether the phone can retain the admitted burst within its byte budget. If any still-authorized reply needs a drop, cancellation or resend, record the measured limit before proposing any host work. Enable the hold only for a load the phone can bound and replay completely. HS `ReplyPlaybackController.swift:128–153,161–175,202–215` · `ReplyPlaybackController+Idle.swift:78–101,115–133` · `ReplyAudioProblem.swift:122–144`.

**Existing host limitations during a silent hold.** On delivery, haild records reply text and audio and estimates a playout window. Matching words can already qualify as echo before the phone schedules a sample. Delivery of a final question also sets the shared `replyAsked` flag. ## 7. Questions for the owner

1. Is five seconds the right point to let **one queued answer** start during sustained speech? A shorter ceiling reduces waiting. A longer ceiling protects more of a dictated note.
2. For the first word, should a fresh unlabelled onset receive the proposed provisional protection even if a nearby person can occasionally delay an answer? Waiting for verified ownership leaves the start of some thoughts uncovered.
3. Should AirPods use the same timing as the speaker? The proposal keeps both at five seconds and preserves the selected output.
4. Ordinary acknowledgements keep baseline timing in phase 1. Should a later phase let them wait once the host can distinguish them from confirmations? For deliberate Replay, the proposed default is immediate playback subject to privacy and Talk.
5. If several fragments each earned an answer, is playing each admitted answer in FIFO order acceptable? This design retains them all. Combining or withdrawing superseded answers would be a separate decision about requests.
