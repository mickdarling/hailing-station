CoachingChildTransport and RightyoCoachingLaunch, not a second transcript runtime or network client.

and an absolute readable regular authored catalog of 1..8192 bytes; mock suggestions cannot be combined

owned-child retirement remain unchanged. No ordinary child opts into this capability implicitly.

Jev, TLS, credentials, models, native audio factories, devices or installed speech services. The sealed
qualified b244 synthetic launcher remains mock-only and rejects hosted/native execution. Actual command
